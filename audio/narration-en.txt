In Tallapaka village, a child was born to a childless couple, a gift of Lord Vishnu himself. As a young man, Annamayya saw the beauty of Lord Venkateswara, and his heart was captured forever. He walked the seven holy hills to Tirumala, and there he stayed, pouring his love into thousands of hymns. When a king demanded songs in his own praise, Annamayya refused. He would sing only for the Lord. Thrown into prison, he called upon Narasimha, and the iron bars burst into flames. His songs were saved on copper plates, and they still echo in Tirumala today, a voice of pure devotion that never fades.
